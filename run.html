Rún

A private messenger. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and routed over the Tor network — no accounts, no phone number, no tracking.

Rún keeps your conversations on your device, inside an encrypted vault. The relay that passes messages along never sees your IP address and only ever holds sealed, fixed-size ciphertext it cannot read. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

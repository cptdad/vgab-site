About the company

Vikstrom Group AB is a Swedish limited company (aktiebolag), registered in Sweden. We build privacy-first software — applications designed so that strong end-to-end encryption and minimal data collection are core requirements rather than afterthoughts.

Our current product is Rún, an end-to-end encrypted messenger that routes its traffic over the Tor network and keeps no user accounts and no logs. We develop, publish, and support it ourselves.
